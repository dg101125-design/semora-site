The comparison

How does Managed Growth Pod compare with hiring a marketing manager?

Managed Growth Pod is priced against a hire, not against an agency. Shared comes to $88,800 a year, under a Marketing Specialist on $80k–$95k plus super. Held comes to $162,000, under a Head of Marketing on $172,500 plus super. Both salary figures are from SEEK's published Melbourne marketing salary data, 2026. What a salary also buys is somebody in the building, which a retainer does not, so the fair comparison is the list above against one person's week.
