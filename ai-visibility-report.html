Does AI know you exist?

Ask ChatGPT who does what you do. If the answer names your competitors and not you, that is where your next client just went. We will run the check properly and send you what we find.

01 / What you get

A real report.
Not a widget.

We take the questions a buyer in your field actually asks, run them on ChatGPT and Perplexity in clean sessions with the wording fixed in advance, and write down exactly what came back — the same instrument we score ourselves with every month, results published, zeros included. No demo screen. No canned insights.

The report shows whether AI mentions you, cites you or recommends you, names the competitors appearing in the answers where you should be, and ends with the three fixes we would make first, in order. It arrives by email. A document you keep, whether or not we ever speak again.

One thing it will not contain is a guarantee of placement. Nobody controls what these systems answer, and anyone who promises a position in them is selling you weather.

02 / How we run it

Every question is asked in a fresh session with no account signed in, because a logged-in run measures what the platform knows about the asker, not what a stranger would be told. The wording is fixed before we start and never adjusted to flatter the result. Each answer is scored on three rungs. Mentioned means your name appears. Cited means the answer links to you. Recommended means you are the suggestion, which is the only rung that wins work.

The report lands in your inbox within 24 hours, sent by a named person. No call unless you ask for one.
