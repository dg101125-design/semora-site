How does a SEMORA STUDIO engagement actually run?

In order: the Proof Check in weeks 0–2 sets the baseline, Commercial Growth Signal in weeks 3–6 sets the position and the one agreed number, Growth Foundation Build builds the surfaces in weeks 7–16, and Managed Growth Pod runs the marketing monthly from month 5. The walk-through below is illustrative, written for a three-partner buyer's advocacy — not a client case. Real cases will publish to the proof standard: named, numbered, dated, caveated.

Weeks 0–2
Proof Check

The baseline, on the record

The practice scores 21/44. Weakest surfaces: named results (0), fee explanation (1), first screen (1). Baseline: one win in four qualified enquiries, the founder in every first call. Fee: $1,000, credited.

Weeks 3–6
Commercial Growth Signal

The position, and the number

Position: the advocate for time-poor professional purchasers. Fixed fee, with the reasoning written for the website. The contract number: win rate on qualified enquiries.

Weeks 7–16
Growth Foundation Build

The surfaces, built

Six-page site with the fee page and position on screen one. Proposal system. Enquiry response standard — answered by the advocate, same day. Every source file handed over at completion.

Month 5 →
Managed Growth Pod / Shared

The engine, running

Two editorial pieces and six campaign assets a month. Two proposals built. Monthly reporting against the win rate. The whole thing costs the founder one hour a week.
