Reviews, results and evidence

What we can actually prove.

It comes down to two questions. Who else have you done this for, and can I ring them? SEMORA STUDIO began operating in 2026, so the honest answer is short. We will not manufacture a longer one. Everything we can prove is on this page, sorted into four kinds of evidence, so you can see which is which.

01 / Verified client reviews

Are there verified SEMORA STUDIO client reviews?

None yet, and we will say so. A verified review, to SEMORA STUDIO, means one thing. A real client, in their own words, with a name, a role, a date and written permission to print it. We have none. Not one. And we will not dress up something weaker to fill the gap, so you will not find praise here from a client we decline to name, or a tidy line we wrote that a client nodded at, or five stars signed with a single initial. We ask at the 90-day review.

Client words land on this page, or they land nowhere.

Regulated health services are the harder case. The National Law prohibits clinical testimonials in advertising, so we will not publish one at all, and that binds us exactly as it binds the clinic.

02 / Measured client results

How does SEMORA STUDIO publish a client result?

To a six-part template, published early and listed below: the baseline, the intervention, the result, the period, the caveats and the client's written permission. A written case study publishes only when it has all six. Miss one and Jennifer Wong holds it back. The headline figures on the home page are the short form of live engagements, and the six-part write-up behind each one lands here as the client signs off the detail. It is the same proof standard the rest of the method runs on.

The baseline

What was true before we started, measured, with the date

The intervention

What we actually did — scope, not adjectives

The result

The agreed number, measured the same way as the baseline

The period

Start and end dates, so you can judge the sample

The caveats

What else changed, what we did not control, what the number excludes

The permission

Named and approved by the client, or it does not appear

Proof,not promises.
