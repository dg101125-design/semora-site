Method

Accountable to one number. In writing.

A named metric in the contract, everything handed over at completion — and our own instrument applied to us, in public.

How does SEMORA STUDIO work?

01

What is the one number SEMORA STUDIO is accountable to?

One metric from your audit baseline — win rate, fee integrity, or founder dependency — reviewed at 90 days, on the record. The full accountability clause is in legal drafting and publishes here when signed off: a commitment before it is enforceable would be a claim we could not evidence.

02

What do you own at the end of an engagement?

Everything SEMORA STUDIO built for you. Source files, tokens, templates and the written governance model transfer at completion, by contract. SIL OFL fonts — no seat licences. Nothing held hostage to a retainer.

03

Why hire a marketing agency that has no track record yet?

SEMORA STUDIO is new, and says so, unprompted. What substitutes for tenure: named people, this published method, published prices, and source files that transfer by contract — if we vanished tomorrow, you keep everything.
