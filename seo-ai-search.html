01

The shift

Search has two doors now.

Your buyer still types into Google. What comes back, more and more, is an answer that names its sources.

The ranked list

Competes for placement

The buyer
Types a phrase
Comes back
A list of pages
Decided by
Relevance · authority · speed
Winning is
Your page near the top

The generated answer

Competes for trust

The buyer
Asks a full question
Comes back
An answer citing sources
Decided by
Entity clarity · evidence
Winning is
Your firm, named

Most sites are built for the first door and absent from the second. Not ranked low. Absent.

02

The method

Five steps, one order.

No baseline, no work. If it cannot be measured, we write that down before we start.

  1. 01

    Technical audit

    Platform, speed, what Google has indexed, whether the architecture can rank. Ranked by what it costs you.

  2. 02

    Keyword research

    What buyers type, not what the industry calls itself. One term, one owner page.

  3. 03

    Technical fixes

    Worked in order, indexability first. Every fix closed by re-measurement.

  4. 04

    On-page

    Title, headings, first hundred words, links, alt text, schema. Each page built for the term it owns.

  5. 05

    Content and authority

    Evergreen converts. Editorial earns citations. Original research and digital PR, never bought links.

After step five: one commercial number, monthly. Rankings explain it. They never get to be it.
