01 / Why patients choose a clinic

Patients decide before
the first appointment.

They cannot judge clinical quality — so they judge everything around it. The website's first screen. How hard it is to book. Whether every practitioner presents like the same clinic. What the gap payment is, and whether anyone explains it before they ask.

Those proxies are not marketing decoration. They are the actual decision criteria — for patients choosing a clinic, and for the referring GPs who send them. Most clinics have never audited them, because they assume they are being chosen on the care.

They are being chosen on the surfaces around the care. That is what this budget works on.

What this document is — and is not

This is an engagement plan: exactly what Southern River Clinic receives each month, at published prices and published quantities. It is not a case study — we do not present results we have not earned, and any projection in these pages is marked Illustrative. Our first obligation to you is the same standard we would demand of your clinic: sourced, dated, baselined, attributable.

05 / The rules we will not break

AHPRA is not a footnote.
It is part of the service.

Advertising rules for regulated health services bind your clinic — and therefore bind everything we produce for it. Knowing these rules is part of what you are paying for.

  • No testimonials about clinical services. This is a hard prohibition — we exclude review and testimonial automation for regulated services entirely, and build your proof from what is allowed.
  • No claims that create unreasonable expectation of benefit. Every editorial piece and asset is checked against this before it ships.
  • No offers that encourage indiscriminate use of a regulated service.
  • TGA rules applied to any therapeutic goods referenced.
  • Your compliance sign-off, in writing, before anything publishes — built into the approval flow, not bolted on.

Where a clinic's proof legally lives

Practitioner credentials and training · facility and equipment · wait times · continuity of practitioner · referral relationships. That is the evidence we build with — it is compliant, and to a referring GP it is more persuasive than any review widget.
