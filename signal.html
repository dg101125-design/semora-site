The price

Why does Commercial Growth Signal cost $3,000?

A position is fee infrastructure. Measure the $3,000 against what an undefended one costs you in a year. Open last year's client list and total the gap between each fee you quoted and the fee you took. That is one undefended year. It opens again next year while the conversation behind those discounts stays the same. For most practices the sum is a multiple of this fee.

Six objections come up on a first call, and you answer them differently each time, because nobody wrote down the best version. The position, the fee logic and the objection playbook exist to end that discount. Check the arithmetic against your own books. That is the only claim we make for it.

What this is not

Not a workshop with sticky notes

Hand the document to whoever starts on Monday. On day one they can repeat it correctly. Written down, or it is not a position.
