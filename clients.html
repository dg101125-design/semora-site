Reviews, results and evidence

What we can actually prove.

SEMORA STUDIO began operating in 2026. We do not claim a long record of independent reviews, and we will not manufacture one. This page separates four different kinds of evidence so you can see exactly which is which.

Kind of evidenceWhat it means Status today
Verified client reviews Direct client words, published with written permission and a name None yet
Measured client results Baseline, what we changed, the outcome, the period, and the caveats First one at the 90-day review
Named engagements Work we are contracted to deliver, named with permission 1 — AXIS
Illustrative examples Hypothetical, written by us to show a format. Not testimonials Labelled as samples

01 / Verified client reviews

None yet —
and we will say so.

A verified review means a real client, quoted in their own words, published with written permission, with their name and role attached and the date shown.

We have none to publish yet. Client words are collected at the 90-day review, and they appear here or nowhere — we do not publish anonymous praise, paraphrased quotes or initials-only testimonials.

For regulated health services we will not publish clinical testimonials at all: the National Law prohibits them in advertising, and that applies to us as much as to the clinic.

02 / Measured client results

The template,
published early.

Every result we publish will carry all six of these. If we cannot supply one of them, the result does not publish — which is why this section is still empty.

The baseline

What was true before we started, measured, with the date

The intervention

What we actually did — scope, not adjectives

The result

The agreed number, measured the same way as the baseline

The period

Start and end dates, so you can judge the sample

The caveats

What else changed, what we did not control, what the number excludes

The permission

Named and approved by the client, or it does not appear

Proof,not promises.
