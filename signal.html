Why it is priced like this

A position is
fee infrastructure.

Run your own numbers: last year's engagements, times the discounts you gave to close them. That sum — repeated every year — is what an undefended fee costs. For most practices it is a multiple of this fee.

The position, the fee logic and the objection playbook exist to end that discount. Check the arithmetic against your own books — that is the only claim we make for it.

What this is not

Not a workshop with sticky notes.

The deliverable is a document a new hire can repeat correctly on day one. A position that cannot survive being written down is not a position.
