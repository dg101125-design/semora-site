How does a SEMORA STUDIO engagement actually run?

In order: the Proof Audit in weeks 0–2 sets the baseline, SIGNAL / in weeks 3–6 sets the position and the one agreed number, / HAUS builds the surfaces in weeks 7–16, and MOMENTUM runs the marketing monthly from month 5. The walk-through below is illustrative, written for a three-partner buyer's advocacy — not a client case. Real cases will publish to the proof standard: named, numbered, dated, caveated.

Weeks 0–2
The Proof Audit

The baseline, on the record

The practice scores 21/44. Weakest surfaces: named results (0), fee explanation (1), first screen (1). Baseline: one win in four qualified enquiries, the founder in every first call. Fee: $3,000, credited.

Weeks 3–6
SIGNAL /

The position, and the number

Position: the advocate for time-poor professional purchasers. Fixed fee, with the reasoning written for the website. The contract number: win rate on qualified enquiries.

Weeks 7–16
/ HAUS

The surfaces, built

Six-page site with the fee page and position on screen one. Proposal system. Enquiry response standard — answered by the advocate, same day. Every source file handed over at completion.

Month 5 →
MOMENTUM / Shared

The engine, running

Two editorial pieces and six campaign assets a month. Two proposals built. Monthly reporting against the win rate. The whole thing costs the founder one hour a week.
