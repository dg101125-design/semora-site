Method

How we work

01

The number

One metric from your audit baseline — win rate, fee integrity, or founder dependency — reviewed at 90 days, on the record. The full accountability clause is in legal drafting and publishes here when signed off: a commitment before it is enforceable would be a claim we could not evidence.

02

The handover

Source files, tokens, templates and the written governance model transfer at completion. SIL OFL fonts — no seat licences. Nothing held hostage to a retainer.
