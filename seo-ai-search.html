04 / How the work is done

The Semora SEO system. We show you how it is measured.

An agency will show you the result and keep the working. Here is the working: what runs every month, what each step has to produce before it counts as done, and whose name is on the report by the time it reaches you. It is not a secret.

Monthly operating report How it is produced

  1. 01

    Measure before anything moves

    Access first: Search Console and analytics, both in your own name. The business profile follows. Then the buyer questions go to the machines in clean sessions, with the wording written down before the first run.

    Closes on a baseline you can read, with the date on it.

  2. 02

    Diagnose, and rank by cost

    Four checks: platform, speed, indexation, architecture. Each fault is ranked by what it is costing you, which is a different order from what is quickest to fix.

    Closes on a fix ledger, sorted, with an owner per row.

  3. 03

    Fix in order

    Indexability first, because a page a machine cannot reach earns nothing downstream. Your developer can ship the work, or we can.

    Closes on the re-measurement of that row, dated.

  4. 04

    Report the commercial number

    The first line is enquiries, pipeline or new clients. Rankings and citations sit underneath it, as the explanation.

    Closes on next month, held against this one.

Why it holds

  • A row closes when the re-measurement says so, and the date goes beside it.
  • The question wording is fixed before the first run, so a month that moved can be told apart from a month that was asked differently.
  • Misses stay on the sheet. A scoreboard that hides its zeros cannot be read next month.
  • A second reader re-runs the checks. What they stop is written into the report rather than quietly repaired.

06 / Questions we get asked

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO earns a place on the list of results. GEO, generative engine optimisation, earns a place inside the generated answer, as a named source. Same buyer, two doors; the second is decided by entity clarity and evidence rather than by links alone.

Can you guarantee a position in an AI answer?

No. Nobody can. No platform sells one, and an agency that offers one is selling something else. What can be worked is whether you are eligible, whether a machine can resolve your name to one company, and whether there is evidence for it to cite.

How do you measure AI search visibility?

Sixteen questions your buyer would ask, put to ChatGPT and Perplexity in clean sessions with the wording fixed in advance. Each answer scores 0 to 3: not mentioned, mentioned, cited, recommended. The total is reported monthly, misses included.

Do you need access to our Search Console?

Yes, before anything starts. Without it an audit is a guess dressed as a diagnosis: we can see what your site is, but not what it earns. Analytics and the business profile follow the same rule.

What does the first month look like?

Access first: Search Console, analytics, the business profile, and whoever holds the site, all in your name before anything else happens, because an audit without them is a guess dressed as a diagnosis. Then the baseline, written down. Then the four-part audit of platform, speed, indexation and architecture, which produces the fix ledger, sorted by what each fault costs you and closed row by row on re-measurement. Each row has a date. The keyword-to-page map and the question-to-page map come out of the same month. The first report follows the baseline, so the number on its first line has a starting figure beside it and a date under it.

Do you work with our own developer?

Yes. Technical SEO research and support is built for that: we find the faults, rank them by cost and specify the fix; your team ships it; we re-measure and close the row.

Which platforms do you optimise for?

Five surfaces: Google’s ranked results and map pack, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot. One body of evidence serves all five; what differs is how each one reads it.

Is this priced?

Yes, in figures, on the quotation page and the homepage. A quote is arithmetic on published numbers. The budget conversation is already on the page.
