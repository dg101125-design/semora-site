The instrument

The eleven surfaces,
and the question each one answers.

Somebody who has a problem they have not told anyone about yet checks these, roughly in this order, before they ever speak to you, and by the time the phone rings most of the checking is already done. You do not see any of it. Each is scored 0–4 with the evidence recorded beside it. Score yourself first, free →

The order of work

How long does the Proof Audit take, and what do you have to do?

Two weeks, and ninety minutes of your time. You give us two things: one 90-minute working session, and your last five proposals. Nothing else is asked of you. Nothing waits on your team.

Then, in order. We score the eleven surfaces against evidence, and we set your baseline: win rate on qualified enquiries, average fee, founder-hours per win. Then your proposals. Your last five, read against the ones that won. Then the three competitors you name, scored on the same instrument, and if that list is the wrong one, if the firm actually taking your work is not on it, we say so and we say why. Last comes the part you actually wanted: the three surfaces to fix, ranked, with the reason each one sits where it does.

It ends with a 60-minute findings session, recorded, and a written report. The recording and the report are yours.

Why it is paid

Why does SEMORA STUDIO charge for an audit when others give one away?

Because a free audit is a sales call in a costume, and specialists can tell. Charging makes it an engagement rather than a pitch, and an engagement is the version whose findings you can actually use, because nothing is held back for a proposal at the end. That is the trade. It also filters for people who are actually going to act on them.

You keep the report whether or not you hire us.

The $3,000 is credited in full against any engagement you start within 90 days. Hire us and the audit costs nothing. Walk away and you still hold a scored baseline, and a ranked list of what to fix in what order, which is more than you had on the Monday morning you first picked up the phone to us. That is the deal.

What it is not

What is the Proof Audit not?

Not a strategy, and not a proposal. It measures where you stand. It does not decide your position, write your messaging or build anything, and if you want the audit to hand you a strategy at the end you will be disappointed. That is deliberate. That work is SIGNAL / and / HAUS, priced separately and published in the same detail.

It is also optional. You can start at SIGNAL / instead and we set the baseline there in week one. The rule underneath all of it is no work without a baseline, because otherwise nobody can say at 90 days whether anything moved. That rule does not bend.
