Why it is priced like this

Why does SIGNAL / cost $14,000?

Because a position is fee infrastructure, and the $14,000 is measured against what an undefended position costs you in a year. Open last year's client list. Beside each name, write the fee you first quoted and the fee you actually took to win the work. Add up the gap. That is what one undefended year costs you, and the same gap opens again this year, and the year after that, because nothing about the conversation that produced those discounts has changed since the last time you had it. Nobody enjoys that sum. For most practices it is a multiple of this fee. Run it in your own books before you talk to us.

Six objections come up on a first call, and you answer them a different way each time, because nobody ever wrote down the best version. That is the leak. The position, the fee logic and the objection playbook exist to end that discount. Check the arithmetic against your own books. That is the only claim we make for it.
