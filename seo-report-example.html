Specimen · every figure invented

The monthly report, opened

This is the shape of the report a client receives, filled with invented figures so you can read the structure without reading anyone’s business, and the failures sit beside the wins for a reason that has more to do with arithmetic than with honesty: by month three somebody has to decide where the next month’s hours go, and a page carrying only good news gives them nothing to decide with. So the weak layers are named. So is the work that has not started, and the items sitting with the client rather than with us.

Reporting period: month three of an engagement · Prepared by SEMORA STUDIO · Reviewed by a second reader, independent of delivery

01 / The judgement

Seven layers, scored

Each layer scores on what can be shown, rather than on effort spent. A layer scores low when the evidence is missing, even where the work is done. That is the point of scoring it.

Technical foundation92
Keyword system85
Content system65
Conversion tracking60
Crawling and indexing55
Entity recognition45
External authority25

The weakest layer is the one an answer engine reads first. Naming it in month three, in writing, is how it gets fixed by month six. Leaving it out costs a quarter.

02 / The score

Two numbers, not one

Building the system and getting a commercial result out of it are different things. Averaging them into a single number hides which one you are paying for this month.

72/100

System build. Infrastructure, process gates, and tools a stranger could re-run to check the claim.

62/100

Result readiness. Weighted across the seven layers: how far the system is from being cited by a machine and bringing work in.

Why the second number sits lower: the citation work has not started, the profile verification is outstanding, part of the site waits to be re-crawled, and this month’s visibility run has not been taken, so a claim that things improved would rest on air. We do not make one.

03 / The register

Every item, and its state

The register is the spine of the engagement. Each row closes on evidence a second reader can re-run. Items waiting on you are marked, so none of them sits in the dark.

  • Closed
    Indexing status

    Console export reconciled against the sitemap; the arithmetic checks out.

  • Closed
    Core Web Vitals

    Home page lifted from red to green; largest paint cut by two thirds.

  • Closed
    Keyword-to-page map

    One owner page per term, primary and supporting named per cluster.

  • Partial
    Buyer question list

    Mapped from research. The real enquiry wording fills in as enquiries arrive.

  • Your action
    Business profile verification

    Only the owner can complete it, and it blocks the entity cross-check below.

  • Blocked
    Entity cross-check

    Waiting on the item above. The site side is already done.

  • Open
    External citation programme

    The target list can be drafted here. Sending it is yours to authorise.

  • Open
    First visibility benchmark

    Runs on the locked protocol, and needs the sessions captured by hand.

04 / The check

What the second reader stopped

Delivery does not sign off its own work. A second reader re-runs the scripts, samples the register for honesty, and compares the log against the commit history. What they stop is published here.

  • The closed-item count

    The headline count could not be reproduced. The register’s true figures replaced it everywhere.

  • A published boundary statement

    It breached the writing standard. Rewritten, redeployed, confirmed live the same day.

Both were fixed before the report was issued. A month with nothing stopped usually means nobody looked.
